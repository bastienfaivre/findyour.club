# Strategic Improvement Plan — Clashware Platform
*Generated 2026-03-10 — 7-Framework Analysis*

---

## STRATEGIC BRIEF

Clashware is a pre-launch, multilingual web directory that helps people discover and join local clubs, associations, and social activity groups — sports, cultural, social. It's built for two audiences: **visitors looking for a club to join** and **club administrators who want a free, simple web presence**. The platform is donation-funded, free for all clubs, and currently positioned as "the Yellow Pages for social activities." The product itself is solid — clean, functional, well-structured club pages with exactly the right information (schedule, how to join, contact, photos). But the messaging wrapping this product is clinical, featureless, and almost entirely devoid of emotion, story, or identity. The platform has no name, no logo, no domain, no visual identity, and no social proof. It is an excellent engine with no paint job, no license plate, and no driver behind the wheel.

---

## 1. PURPOSE — Why This Exists
*(Framework: Start With Why)*

**Diagnosis**: The current messaging leads firmly with **WHAT** ("an open directory for sports clubs and associations") and touches lightly on **HOW** ("we only show what matters"). The **WHY** — the belief that drives this project — is buried in the About page as a mission statement that reads like a press release. The homepage communicates function, not purpose.

The About page's best line — *"Our vision is a world where finding a local club is as easy as searching for a restaurant"* — is actually the WHY, but it's hidden on a secondary page that most visitors will never see.

**Core WHY** (one sentence):
> "We believe every community deserves to be found — not just the ones with big budgets or social media followings."

**Recommended homepage opening line**:
> "Every town has clubs you've never heard of. We're changing that."

**What to change**:

| Location | Before | After |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Homepage hero subtitle | "Stop spending hours browsing dozens of club websites just to figure out how to join." | "Every town has clubs you've never heard of. We're changing that." |
| About page opener | "We created this platform because we believe every community deserves visibility." | "In every town, there are people meeting every week to do things they love — hiking, chess, yoga, singing. Most of them are invisible online. We built this to fix that." |
| Meta description | "Find your club — the open directory for sports clubs and associations. Browse by activity, region, and discover how to join." | "Every town has clubs you've never heard of. Search by activity, region, or sport — and find your next community." |

## 2. MESSAGE — Making It Stick
*(Framework: Made to Stick — SUCCESs)*

| Dimension | Status | What's Wrong | Concrete Fix |
|-----------|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Simple** | Strong | "Find your club" is clear. But 4 messages compete on homepage (tagline + philosophy + stats + countries). | Keep "Find your club." Ensure ONE message dominates. Strip competing messages. |
| **Unexpected** | Missing | Zero surprise. Homepage confirms expectations. No curiosity gap. | Add knowledge gap opener: "There are 47 clubs within 20km of you right now. You've never heard of most of them." |
| **Concrete** | Weak | Rotating words add variety but no concreteness. No real scenarios. | Replace philosophy line with concrete vignette: "Thomas moved to Lausanne and spent three weeks googling 'badminton club near me'. He found us on a Tuesday, joined a club on Thursday." |
| **Credible** | Missing | No social proof. No testimonials. No "used by." | Testable credibility: "Browse the directory right now — every club is real, reviewed, and verified by our team." Add "Manually verified" badge on club cards. |
| **Emotional** | Missing | About page reads like a corporate statement. Homepage feels like a search engine. | Add WIIFY: For visitors: "Your next community is one click away." For clubs: "Your club deserves more than a Facebook page." Use "community" 10x more than "directory." |
| **Story** | Missing | PRD has three vivid user journeys (Marie, Thomas, Ahmed) — NONE appear on the website. | Add Thomas's story to homepage: 3 sentences + CTA. "Thomas moved to Lausanne and didn't know anyone. He found a badminton club through our directory and showed up the following Thursday. [Browse clubs near you →]" |

**Rewritten core message**:
- **Before**: "Stop spending hours browsing dozens of club websites just to figure out how to join."
- **After**: "Your next community is already meeting this week. You just haven't found them yet."

## 3. POSITIONING
*(Frameworks: Built to Last + Essentialism)*

**Core Ideology**:
- **Values** (3):
  1. **Visibility for all** — every club deserves to be found, regardless of budget or tech skill
  2. **Signal over noise** — we show what matters and nothing else
  3. **Free forever for clubs** — this is a public good, not a business

- **Purpose**: "To make every local community findable — so that no one stays alone in a new town because they didn't know what was meeting around the corner."

**BHAG**: "Every club in Europe listed and findable within 5 years — the definitive directory for community life."

**Essential Focus**: "The place where you go to find a club near you."

**What to cut**:
1. **"Open directory"** — replace with "the home for local clubs"
2. **Stats on homepage** — at pre-launch scale, tiny or abstract numbers hurt more than help
3. **Philosophy line** ("Here, we only show what matters") — design principle, not user message. Move to About.

**Positioning statement**:
> For anyone who just moved somewhere — or anyone who's been meaning to try something new — [Platform Name] is the community finder that shows you every club near you with exactly what you need: what they do, when they meet, and how to join. Unlike Meetup (events, not clubs), Facebook Groups (noisy, unsearchable), or Google (websites, not answers), we show only what matters.

## 4. VIRALITY POTENTIAL
*(Framework: Contagious — STEPPS)*

| Element | Present? | Score | What to Add or Change |
|---------|----------|-------|----------------------|
| **Social Currency** | No | 2/10 | Reframe discovery as insider knowledge. Add shareable club cards with "Share this club" button generating branded preview card. |
| **Triggers** | No | 1/10 | Own the trigger: **"New in town?"** Put it on homepage and all marketing. Every time someone says "I just moved," the response should be "Check [Platform]." |
| **Emotion** | Partial | 3/10 | Core emotion should be hope + belonging: "there's a community waiting for you." Currently communicates convenience, not belonging. |
| **Public** | No | 1/10 | Make "Powered by [Platform]" more visible. Add "Found on [Platform]" badge. Consider embeddable "Find us on [Platform]" widgets. |
| **Practical Value** | Partial | 4/10 | Create "Club info card" — single-image summary easy to forward via WhatsApp/text. |
| **Stories** | No | 0/10 | Zero stories anywhere. See Section 2. |

**Virality Score**: 1.5/6 — Low. Will not spread organically without fundamental changes.

**Top priority fix**: Generate dynamic OG images per club page. Every shared link should unfurl into a branded card (club photo, name, activity, location, platform logo).

## 5. ADOPTION PATH
*(Framework: The Tipping Point)*

**Bottleneck**: Stickiness. The platform doesn't yet have a message sticky enough to survive 24 hours of retelling.

**Connector play**: Municipal officials, HR/relocation consultants, expat community leaders. Give them a branded link per city and one-line pitch.

**Maven play**: Local sports journalists, community-minded tech bloggers, club presidents. Give them early access and the founder story.

**Salesman pitch**:
> "You know how when you move somewhere new, you want to join a club but there's no easy way to find them? We built a free directory where every club has a clean page with their schedule, how to join, and contact info. Think of it like the Yellow Pages for community life."

**Stickiness fix**: Add a "New in [city]?" landing page per city.

**Broken windows**:
1. No CTA on homepage — rotating headline then... nothing
2. Apply page title "Apply to Join" is confusing
3. No logo, no favicon, no brand color — signals "template" and "unfinished"

## 6. PERSUASION GAPS
*(Framework: Influence — 6 Principles)*

| Principle | State | What to Add or Fix |
|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| **Reciprocity** | Weak | The word "free" — the platform's biggest asset — appears NOWHERE. Say "free" early and often. |
| **Commitment** | Missing | No small first step for visitors. Add a search bar on homepage: "What do you want to do?" |
| **Social Proof** | Missing | Zero social proof. Use "Every club is manually reviewed and verified by our team" as pre-launch proof. |
| **Authority** | Missing | No founder bio, no team page, no human behind it. Add short founder note to About page. |
| **Liking** | Weak | Professional but impersonal. Inject humanity. Use "we" more. Add a photo. Mirror audience language. |
| **Scarcity** | Missing | Use country-by-country rollout as genuine scarcity: "We're launching in Switzerland first — claim your page now." |

**Biggest gap**: Reciprocity. "Free" should appear on homepage, apply page, meta description, About, and footer.

## 7. PRIORITY ACTION PLAN

### Do first — high impact, low effort:

1. **Add "free" to 5 key locations** — homepage, apply page title, meta description, About, footer (1 hour)
2. **Add a CTA button to homepage hero** — "Browse clubs →" or "Find a club near you →" (15 minutes)
3. **Rewrite apply page title** — "List Your Club — It's Free" + subtitle about 10-minute setup (30 minutes)
4. **Add one story to homepage** — Thomas's journey, 3 sentences + CTA (30 minutes)
5. **Rewrite homepage subtitle** — "Every town has clubs you've never heard of. We're changing that." (5 minutes)
6. **Add "Manually verified" badge to club cards** — leverage operator approval as credibility signal (1 hour)

### Do next — high impact, higher effort:

7. **Choose a platform name and secure a domain** — name must pass the "phone test"
8. **Design minimal visual identity** — one brand color, wordmark, favicon
9. **Generate dynamic OG images per club page** — highest-leverage virality mechanic
10. **Rewrite About page** — founder story, not mission statement
11. **Add "How it works" 3-step section to homepage**
12. **Expand activity taxonomy** — add music, theater, dance, martial arts, running, cycling, tennis, volleyball, basketball, table tennis, badminton, climbing, board games

### Defer or stop:

- **Support/Donate page** — keep as-is until post-launch with traffic
- **Map view** in search — high effort, not a conversion driver
- **Blog/content marketing** — product IS the content pre-launch
- **FAQ page** — build reactively after launch

## REWRITTEN ARTIFACTS

### 1. Homepage headline + subheadline

**Before**:
- Headline: "Find your [football team / yoga class / hiking group / ...]"
- Subtitle: "Stop spending hours browsing dozens of club websites just to figure out how to join."
- Philosophy: "Here, we only show what matters — nothing more."

**After**:
- Headline: "Find your [football team / yoga class / hiking group / ...]" *(keep)*
- Subtitle: "Every town has clubs you've never heard of. We make them findable."
- CTA button: **"Browse clubs →"**
- Below CTA: "Free for all clubs. Manually verified. No noise."

### 2. One-line pitch (max 20 words)
> "Find your next club — sports, arts, social. Every club listed free, with schedule, photos, and how to join."

### 3. Positioning tagline
> "Every club, findable."

### 4. Apply page rewrite

**Before**: "Apply to Join" / "Submit your association's application to join our platform."

**After**: "Get Your Club Online — Free" / "Set up a beautiful club page in 10 minutes. Photos, schedule, how to join — everything a visitor needs. No cost, no catch."

### 5. About page rewrite

> **Why we built this**
>
> I moved to a new city and wanted to join a volleyball club. Simple, right?
>
> I spent three weeks searching. Google gave me outdated websites. Facebook groups were chaos. The municipality had a PDF list from 2019. I eventually found a club through a friend of a friend — and when I showed up, they said "we get this all the time, people just can't find us."
>
> That's when I realized: the clubs exist. The people exist. What's missing is the bridge.
>
> [Platform Name] is that bridge. A free, clean directory where every club has a page with exactly what you need to know: what they do, when they meet, and how to join. No ads, no algorithms, no noise.
>
> We're funded by donations and built by a small team that believes every community deserves to be found — not just the ones with big budgets or social media followings.
>
> If you're looking for a club, search the directory. If you run one, [list it for free →].
