Strategic Improvement Plan v2 — Club Directory Platform
Generated 2026-03-10 — 7-Framework Analysis (Corrected)

STRATEGIC BRIEF
This is a pre-launch, multilingual directory that solves two problems at once: people waste absurd amounts of time trying to find basic information about local clubs (schedule, how to join, contact), and clubs themselves — especially small ones — are invisible online because they lack the budget, skills, or motivation to maintain a web presence. The platform gives every club a clean, standardized page for free, and gives every visitor a fast way to find what's near them. It has no name, no logo, no domain, and no visual identity yet — but the product itself is already strong: well-structured club pages, multilingual SEO, and a clean UX that gets out of the way. The founder is a solo developer doing this as volunteer work, launching first in French-speaking Switzerland, with a donation-funded model and full cost transparency. The messaging challenge is nuanced: visitors need the platform to just work silently, while club admins need to be emotionally convinced to spend 10 minutes listing their club. These are two fundamentally different communication jobs, and the current messaging serves neither one well.

1. PURPOSE — Why This Exists
(Framework: Start With Why)

Context screening: Fully applicable. This is a community-driven, mission-based project with a founder whose personal conviction IS the brand. The WHY is the single strongest asset this platform has — and it's currently invisible.

Diagnosis: The messaging leads with WHAT ("an open directory for sports clubs and associations") on the homepage, and touches HOW ("we only show what matters") in the philosophy line. The WHY — a deeply personal belief about human connection and the absurdity of how hard it is to find a club — exists only in the founder's head. It doesn't appear anywhere on the platform.

The v1 analysis proposed a WHY about "every community deserving to be found." That's a subset of the real WHY but misses the founder's actual conviction: people need real-world social connection, and the internet — which should make finding it easy — actively makes it harder.

Core WHY (one sentence):

"We believe people belong together in the real world — and the internet shouldn't be the obstacle standing between someone and their next community."

5. ADOPTION PATH
(Framework: The Tipping Point)

Context screening: Fully applicable. This is a community-driven product that depends on word of mouth and social spread. All three people types (Connectors, Mavens, Salesmen) are relevant. The founder himself is the Salesman (via the video).

Bottleneck diagnosis: Stickiness, but not in the way v1 diagnosed. Club presidents who are digitally active — they influence dozens of other club presidents in their canton/region. Onboard them first; they become ambassadors.

Simple	8/10	"Find your club" is near-perfect. Clean, clear, one action.

Dimension	Score	Assessment
